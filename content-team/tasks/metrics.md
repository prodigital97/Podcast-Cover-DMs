# Metrics

The only place the Analyst may read numbers from. Paste each week's export here
before review day. If a week is missing, the Analyst reports it unread rather
than reasoning from what shipped.

## Format

```
## Week of YYYY-MM-DD

| post | format | reach | saves | shares | avg watch % | 3s retention % |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

Leave a cell blank when the platform does not report it. A blank is readable; a
guess is not.
