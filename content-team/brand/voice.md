# Voice

The Script Writer reads this before writing any caption. This is the "study past
captions first" step made concrete — a model cannot study captions it has never
been shown.

## Paste real captions here

Five to ten captions that sound like you, unedited. These do more work than any
description of your voice, so keep them current and delete ones you have
outgrown.
